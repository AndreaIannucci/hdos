# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-09

### Added

- Batch and online linear regression.
- Cholesky and SVD regression backends.
- Ridge regularisation with an unpenalised intercept.
- Numerically stable training R-squared.
- Running mean and unbiased sample variance.
- Batch and incremental PCA.
- CMake installation and `find_package(hdos)` support.
- Public examples and umbrella header.
- Static-library distribution.
- Cross-platform validation with GCC, Clang, and MSVC.
- AddressSanitizer and UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer testing.
